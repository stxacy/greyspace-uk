Grey Space Recordings

About

Sound built for concrete floors.
Not streaming dashboards.

Grey Space Recordings is a UK underground music collective and label. We started in back rooms and warehouse circuits, and we've stayed there by choice.

We run nights, press records, and give a platform to the sounds that matter. No commercial concessions. No A&R gatekeeping. No genre ceiling.

Our nights span 140, DNB, grime, garage, and everything that lives in the grey between. The only thing that matters is whether it hits in a dark room at 2am.

If you've been there, you already know what we're about.

Label
Grey Space Recordings presses limited runs on vinyl and digital. Every release is intentional. No reissues, no remasters — if you missed it, you missed it.

Nights
Grey Space events are small, dark, and loud. We work with venues that understand what the music needs. Doors are always last minute. Lineups sometimes stay secret.

Community
The collective runs deeper than the label. Residents, DJs, MCs, and selectors who've been building together since before the bookings.
